# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

THE ARK welcomes technical disagreement and rigorous review. Contributors are expected to be respectful, specific and evidence-driven.

Good participation includes:

- critiquing code rather than people;
- explaining trade-offs;
- citing upstream facts;
- making failure modes explicit;
- helping new contributors understand the architecture;
- respecting licensing and attribution.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, targeted abuse, publishing private information or deliberately sabotaging project infrastructure.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-managed community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary to keep technical collaboration productive.

## Engineering culture

Ambition is encouraged. Overclaiming is not.

Say “CI-tested” when something is CI-tested. Say “field-tested” only after a real offline hardware test.
